GENERA   L DESCRIPTION
The  AS6C1008 is a 1,048,57 6-bit low power
CMOS static random access memory organized as
131,072 words b y 8 bits. It is fabricated  using very
high  performance, high rel iability   CMO  S  technology.   Its
standby   current is stable within the range   of
operating  temperature.
The AS6C1008 is well designed for very low power
system applications, a nd particularly well suited fo r
battery  back-up non-volatile memory application.
The AS6C1008 operates from a single power supply 
of 2.7V ~ 5.5V.
