    DIP / Through-Hole PCB Inventory¶

    These parts are for the DIP / through-hole PCB, not the partial-SMT inventory. See Choose a PCB design before buying parts or ordering boards.

    This is the Revision B 85 x 96 mm, four-layer assisted-loader PCB. The generated BOM owns fitted references, values and footprints. Sockets, standoffs and module pin headers are additional assembly/purchase items.

    Semiconductors and sockets¶

    Reference Part Socket/package
    A1 Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W Two soldered 20-pin male headers and two 20-position socket strips
    U1 Z84C0020PEC DIP-40, 15.24 mm socket
    U2 AS6C1008-55PCN DIP-32, 15.24 mm socket; A16 is buffered bank select
    U3 SN74HCT32N DIP-14, 7.62 mm socket
    U4 SN74AHCT244N DIP-20, 7.62 mm socket
    U7 SN74LVC244AN DIP-20, 7.62 mm socket
    U9 SN74AHCT245N DIP-20, 7.62 mm socket
    U10 SN74LVC245AN DIP-20, 7.62 mm socket
    D1 1N5819 DO-41 axial, 10.16 mm pitch; cathode band toward VSYS
    A2 0.91-inch SSD1315 128x32 I2C OLED 38 x 12 mm body, 1x4 2.54 mm female socket; 3.3 V

    The eight core active packages are A1 and seven DIP ICs; A2 is the additional display. Buy four DIP-20 sockets, one DIP-14, one DIP-32 and one DIP-40. Do not interchange AHCT, HCT and LVC families. Verify actual socket-body dimensions and extraction access, not just pin-row spacing.

    The silkscreen inside each DIP outline names the chip with its reference underneath, for example 74AHCT245 / U9 and Z80 CPU / U1. Check the label before fitting the socket and chip, because the fitted part covers it.

    Resistor arrays¶

    All 40 pull branches use six bussed arrays, with no discrete resistors fitted on the PCB.

    References Qty Value/package Purpose/common pin
    RN1, RN2 2 8x10 kOhm bussed, SIP-9 A0-A15 pull-ups; pin 1 +5 V
    RN3 1 8x10 kOhm bussed, SIP-9 Pico D0-D7 pull-downs; pin 1 GND
    RN4 1 8x10 kOhm bussed, SIP-9 Eight CPU control pull-ups; pin 1 +5 V
    RN5 1 4x10 kOhm bussed, SIP-5 GP4/5/6/7 startup pull-ups; pin 1 3.3 V
    RN6 1 4x10 kOhm bussed, SIP-5 GP2/3/9/8 startup pull-downs; pin 1 GND

    Use bussed, not isolated, networks. Verify the common-pin marking and 2.54 mm pitch. RN5 implements breadboard R17-R20 in order; RN6 implements R21-R24 in order. Never fit the corresponding discrete resistors as well. The breadboard inventory still specifies 40 individual resistors for breadboard construction.

    Capacitors¶

    References Qty Value Footprint/rating
    C1-C7 7 100 nF Ceramic disc or monolithic, 2.50-2.54 mm pitch, body up to 5 mm, at least 10 V
    C8-C10 3 22 uF 4 mm radial electrolytic, 2.00 mm pitch, at least 10 V (16 V recommended)
    C11 1 100 uF 6.3 mm radial electrolytic, 2.50 mm pitch, at least 10 V (16 V recommended)

    C8-C11 positive pad 1 is +5 V and negative pad 2 is GND. Retain polarity silkscreen. The silkscreen marks each electrolytic with its value (22u or 100u) under the reference. Confirm purchased body diameters and lead pitches.

    Connectors, display and mechanical hardware¶

    Item Qty Requirement
    J1 USB-C power breakout 1 Pictured 21.6 x 12.7 mm module; all six pins soldered, only VBUS/+5 V and GND connected
    J1 male pin strip 1 1x6, 2.54 mm; solder to module and carrier, 1.0 mm carrier drills; verify pin fit and underside insulation
    J3 auxiliary power/probe header 1 1x2, 2.54 mm: pin 1 +5 V, pin 2 GND; use only one source, J1 or J3
    J2 expansion header 1 1x4, 2.54 mm: GP22/SDA, GP26/SCL, 3.3 V, GND; silkscreen pin labels SDA, SCL, 3V3, GND
    OLED female socket 1 1x4, 2.54 mm; 1.0 mm carrier drills, verify pin fit
    OLED insulating support 1 Support module's far end; determine height with the purchased socket/module
    PCB 1 Four-layer, 1.6 mm FR-4 (GND and +5V inner planes), 85 x 96 mm, internal antenna cutout
    M2.5 standoffs and fasteners 4 sets Four corner 2.7 mm NPTH holes, 77 x 88 mm spacing; hardware no wider than 5.4 mm overall, 5.9 mm courtyard

    Use the pictured USB-C module, not the different 12 x 13.8 mm part description. Viewed as photographed with USB-C at the top, the six pads are CC2, D+, D-, CC1, GND, VBUS. The first four have isolated carrier pads: solder them for support but do not wire them to main-board nets. The breakout itself must retain its two 5.1 kOhm CC sink resistors. No module screw fixings are required.

    The OLED socket order is GND, VCC, SCK/SCL, SDA, bottom-to-top viewed from above. Its body/connector position follows the dimension drawing. No module mounting-hole dimensions or assembled-height dimensions are given. Do not assume the board render includes socket, OLED, USB-C breakout or standoff models.

    Use a regulated +5 V supply rated at least 1 A, preferably current limited. Apply main power before connecting Pico USB and disconnect Pico USB first. J1 and J3 are not isolated from each other: do not connect both supplies. Do not power the populated board from Pico USB alone. See power and startup safety. No PLD programmer is required. Reuse the shared multimeter, oscilloscope and logic-analyzer equipment for PCB-specific qualification through the 20 MHz target.
