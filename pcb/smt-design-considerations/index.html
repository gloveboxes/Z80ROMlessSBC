    Partial-SMT PCB Design Considerations¶

    These constraints apply to the 98.3 x 60 mm board in hardware/kicad-smt/. Do not apply the DIP layout's package, socket or mounting dimensions to it. Both boards implement the same assisted-loader circuit, but require separate physical qualification.

    Replacement SRAM and voltage domains¶

    U2 is IS62C1024AL-35QLI, a 128K x 8, 35 ns SRAM in a 450-mil SOP-32 package. The ISSI datasheet covers the exact QLI part. Its SOP pin assignment matches the AS6C1008-55PCN DIP SRAM used on the other board:

    SRAM pin Connection
    1, NC Unconnected
    2, A16 U4.3, buffered bank select from Pico GP8 through U4.17
    16, GND Common GND
    22, CE1# U4.12, buffered Z80 MREQ#
    24, OE# U3.3, buffered RD# OR buffered boot-read-disable
    29, WE# U4.9, buffered Z80 WR#
    30, CE2 +5V, permanently HIGH
    32, VDD +5V

    The ISSI supply range is 4.5-5.5 V. Its TTL-compatible inputs specify HIGH at 2.2 V minimum and LOW at 0.8 V maximum; the Alliance SRAM uses a 0.7 x VCC HIGH threshold. Existing AHCT buffering on A16 and memory controls is retained. Do not connect Pico GPIOs directly to the 5V buses.

    The 35 ns SRAM has different access, output-enable and hold times from the 55 ns part. In particular, minimum output hold after an address change is 3 ns rather than 10 ns. A faster access specification is not proof of read/write turnaround, CPU setup/hold margin or 20 MHz operation.

    U1/U2/U3/U4/U9 use +5V. U7/U10 use Pico-derived +3V3, with 5.5 V-tolerant inputs facing the Z80 bus and 3.3 V outputs facing the Pico. U9 DIR is HIGH (Pico to bus); U10 DIR is LOW (bus to Pico). The shared pin map gives the complete signal assignments.

    Layer stack and routing¶

    Layer Role
    F.Cu Signals and via-free clock routes
    In1.Cu GND plane, no signal tracks
    In2.Cu +5V plane, no signal tracks
    B.Cu Signals

    The board uses a 1.6 mm FR-4 stack, with 1 oz outer and 0.5 oz inner copper. Default signals are 0.30 mm with 0.20 mm clearance; the clock clearance is 0.30 mm. The +5V/VSYS power netclass uses 0.60 mm tracks and +3V3 uses 0.40 mm. USB-C CC tracks use 0.15 mm. Signal vias are 0.80 mm with 0.40 mm drills. Confirm the manufacturer's actual stack and capabilities.

    The Pico module is reflowed on its castellated pads. Its micro-USB end faces the bottom edge and its antenna lies above an enclosed copper/FR-4 window. A 4 mm FR-4 bridge retains the top edge, with a copper keepout. Do not route across the antenna void or place metal, wiring or enclosure walls in its reserved space. Geometry does not qualify Wi-Fi performance.

    The generator retains the Pico underside landing pads as unconnected copper and keeps routed tracks clear of them. Low-profile SMT parts may sit below the OLED; verify the real module/socket height rather than treating a 3D render as a clearance measurement.

    Measured route lengths¶

    The SMT route report records planar copper paths and layer changes, not measured flight times.

    Source/receiver group Path range
    Z80 to SRAM address 27.01-81.10 mm
    Z80 to SRAM data 25.05-43.44 mm
    Pico-write transceiver to SRAM 43.99-84.51 mm
    SRAM to Pico-read transceiver 35.53-77.73 mm
    Pico to U9 9.65-59.11 mm
    Pico to U10 9.17-44.33 mm

    The Z80 clock path is 39.5 mm and via-free. SRAM CE#/OE#/WE# paths are 36.4/30.9/40.7 mm. Longer paths to inspect during qualification include buffered RD# from U4 to U3 at 88.4 mm with four layer changes, and U9-to-SRAM D0/D1 at 84.5/83.3 mm; D1 has six layer changes. Passing layout budgets is not timing or signal-integrity qualification.

    Assembly preview and mechanical checks¶

    Use the generated JLC CPL, not the uncorrected KiCad positions file. Its corrections are specific to the selected JLC component models:

    Package / part CPL rotation correction Check in JLC preview
    SOIC/SOP ICs +270 degrees U2/U3/U4/U7/U9/U10 pin 1
    SMT electrolytics 0 degrees C8-C11 positive lead on +5V
    USB-C C393939 0 degrees, pad-centred position Mouth toward board edge; CPL position 1.5 mm inboard of KiCad origin
    Pico 2 W C42394205 +270 degrees Micro-USB toward bottom edge, antenna over window
    SMA diode and 0603 parts 0 degrees D1 cathode toward VSYS; correct passive values

    Dry-fit the purchased USB-C receptacle before ordering. Its four plated shell slots are 0.85 mm wide, with 1.9 mm or 1.75 mm lengths. Confirm that the quote includes these shell legs in the SMT assembly. Verify the OLED's socket-tail fit and 5.0 mm socket body.

    Four 2.7 mm NPTH holes accept M2.5 fasteners, with centres 3.2 mm from the board edges and 91.9 x 53.6 mm spacing. Check enclosure hardware, underside lead lengths and support height; these are not the DIP board's mounting coordinates.

    The generator enforces solder-mask bridges, track-to-mask clearance, silkscreen and through-hole-to-SMT spacing. Follow the detailed JLCDFM rules and do not waive DRC warnings to accept a route.

    Power and startup safety¶

    J1 is a power-only USB-C receptacle, not a regulator or USB-PD trigger. CC1 and CC2 each have a 5.1k sink resistor to GND. VBUS feeds the +5V rail; SS14 D1 feeds Pico VSYS with its cathode toward VSYS. Pico VBUS is not connected to the main-board supply.

    J3 shares J1's rail without source isolation: use only one external supply at a time. Pico's regulator is the only +3V3 source for U7/U10, the startup pulls and OLED.

    No USB-only operation

    Apply external +5V before connecting Pico USB, and disconnect Pico USB before removing external power. U9's AHCT245 data pins lack power-off isolation; the VSYS diode does not isolate GPIOs. All fitted 5V ICs must be powered together.

    Both data paths default disabled and boot-read inhibit defaults HIGH. Clock, reset, bank select and I/O release default LOW. Inspect the factory assembly, supply rails, reset/inhibit/enables and pin-1/polarity before running SRAM tests.

    Hardware qualification¶

    Start at 1 MHz, then qualify higher rates separately for this PCB. Check the actual address-valid, CE#/OE#/WE#, read-sampling and write-end edges against the ISSI SRAM and CPU specifications, buffer/gate delays and receive-pin waveforms.

    Test both SRAM banks, bank switching, assisted loading, reset/recovery, I/O WAIT, clock stop/resume, CP/M disk traffic and sustained USB/network operation. Use the DHO814 for analogue levels and timing, and the DSLogic Plus for bus ordering. The breadboard's incremental chip-installation procedure is not an assembly sequence for this populated SMT board.
