    Partial-SMT PCB Inventory and Assembly¶

    These parts are for hardware/kicad-smt/, not the DIP / through-hole inventory. The full SMT BOM owns fitted references, values and footprints. The JLC assembly BOM contains only factory-assembled parts.

    Ref Exact part Package / mounting LCSC
    A1 Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W Reflowed castellated module, RaspberryPi_Pico_W_SMD C42394205
    U2 IS62C1024AL-35QLI SOP-32, 450 mil, 1.27 mm pitch; 128K x 8, 35 ns C397875
    U3 SN74HCT32DR SOIC-14, 3.9 mm body C6781
    U4 SN74AHCT244DWR Wide SOIC-20, 7.5 mm body C2675568
    U7 SN74LVC244ADWR Wide SOIC-20, 7.5 mm body C507250
    U9 SN74AHCT245DWR Wide SOIC-20, 7.5 mm body C94773
    U10 SN74LVC245ADWR Wide SOIC-20, 7.5 mm body C571201
    D1 SS14 SMA Schottky; cathode toward Pico VSYS C2480
    J1 TYPE-C 16PIN power receptacle HRO-pattern USB-C footprint; power only C393939

    Do not substitute HCT, AHCT or LVC families, narrow SOIC packages, or the TSOP SRAM variant. Verify stock and the exact part against the uploaded BOM. No Pico socket strips are fitted to this design.

    Resistors¶

    References Qty Value / package LCSC
    R1-R40 40 10k, 1%, 0603 C25804
    R41, R42 2 5.1k, 1%, 0603 C23186

    The 40 startup/bus pulls have the same electrical roles as the breadboard and DIP board, but use individual SMT resistors rather than bussed arrays:

    References Role Pull rail
    R1-R16 A0-A15 address pull-ups +5V
    R17-R24 Pico D0-D7 pull-downs GND
    R25-R32 BUSREQ#, BUSACK#, MREQ#, IORQ#, RD#, WR#, INT#, NMI# pull-ups +5V
    R33-R36 GP4/GP5/GP6/GP7 startup pull-ups +3V3
    R37-R40 GP2/GP3/GP9/GP8 startup pull-downs GND
    R41, R42 Separate USB-C CC1/CC2 sink pull-downs GND

    SMT references R17-R24 are data pulls, not the breadboard's R17-R24 startup resistors. Match by signal and rail, not by resistor reference. The CC resistors are additional to the 40 bus/startup pulls.

    Capacitors¶

    References Qty Value / rating Package LCSC
    C1-C7 7 100 nF, 50 V, X7R 0603 C14663
    C8-C10 3 22 uF, 35 V SMT electrolytic, 6.3 x 5.4 mm footprint C150379
    C11 1 100 uF, 16 V SMT electrolytic, 6.3 x 5.4 mm footprint C346929

    C1-C4/C6 bypass the +5V devices; C5/C7 bypass the +3V3 LVC devices. C8-C11 positive pad 1 is +5V and negative pad 2 is GND. The 22 uF and 100 uF parts look the same size in JLC's preview; check their part numbers and values, not rendered body size.

    Four hand-fitted items¶

    Ref Part to fit after PCBA Requirement
    U1 Z84C0020PEC Z80 DIP-40, 15.24 mm pin-row spacing; hand-soldered directly to this board
    A2 0.91-inch SSD1315 128x32 OLED and female socket 38 x 12 mm module; 1x4, 2.54 mm socket, 5.0 mm body; 3.3 V
    J2 GPIO expansion header 1x4, 2.54 mm: GP22/SDA, GP26/SCL, 3.3 V, GND
    J3 Auxiliary power/probe header 1x2, 2.54 mm: +5V, GND; use one source, J1 or J3

    The OLED socket order is GND, VCC, SCK/SCL, SDA, bottom-to-top viewed from above. Verify socket-tail fit in the 1.0 mm holes and the module's clearance above the low-profile SMT parts. Do not assume the layout reserves space for a Z80 socket; qualify any change to the specified direct-solder assembly separately.

    Mechanical hardware and equipment¶

    • One four-layer, 1.6 mm FR-4 PCB, 98.3 x 60 mm, with its enclosed antenna cutout.
    • Four M2.5 standoffs/fasteners for the 2.7 mm NPTH holes. Hole centres are 3.2 mm from adjacent edges, at 91.9 x 53.6 mm spacing.
    • An insulating OLED support if required by the purchased socket/module.
    • A regulated, preferably current-limited +5V source and the shared bench instruments.

    Apply external +5V before connecting Pico USB; disconnect Pico USB first. Do not power the populated board from Pico USB alone or connect both J1 and J3 supplies. See power and startup safety.
